WHAT THIS IS
  One night for people who build../..break../..solder../..patch../..press
  record. No keynotes. No cover. Hacker summer camp week, August 2026,
  at the Triple Down Saloon inside the Punk Rock Museum, Las Vegas NV.
  Exact date posts on the front page first.

FILES
  index.html      the page. loads raw, no ceremony.
  connect.html    the ceremony. simulated 14.4 dial-in, Mosaic and all.
  sounds/         real modem handshakes (see CREDITS)
  guestbook.txt   the wall. appended by hand.
  changes.txt     maintenance notes.
  README.1ST.txt  you are here.

HOW TO STEAL IT
  1. Take the whole site:
     https://github.com/adambregenzer/mohawklesstalk/archive/refs/heads/main.zip
     or clone it: https://github.com/adambregenzer/mohawklesstalk
  2. Open index.html in a text editor. It is one file on purpose.
  3. Replace our name, dates, and mail link with yours.
  4. Put it on any static host. No build step. No database. No permission.
  5. Mail the address of your mirror to sysop@mohawklesstalk.org.

HOW TO GET ON THE WALL
  Mail one paragraph to sysop@mohawklesstalk.org, subject SIGN THE WALL.
  Handle, where you dial in from, what you're bringing, what band name
  you're claiming. Entries are appended by hand, most Sunday nights.
