THE SIGNAL

SIGNAL../..NOISE is one night for people who build../..break../..solder../..patch../..press record. No keynotes. No cover. Vibe with humans. Trade what you made for what they made.

The theme this year: the moment the scene got a modem. 1985 to 1994, when a local phone number could be a venue and the whole net felt like a show about to start. every homepage was a zine. every download was a dub. everybody you met was building something wild and nobody had asked permission to build it. people will tell you that feeling was naive. wrong. it was a spec, and we are still implementing it.

BRING YOUR OWN

Demos and talks are DIY. carried in by attendees, like every good show. five minutes or fifty../..hardware or holler. if you can explain it in a hallway you can demo it here. some seeds:

  • first hack, first show. the origin story set
  • tools of rebellion. what you build with, and why
  • local echo. what your corner of the scene is making that nobody's heard yet
  • analog heart, digital soul. anything with tape, wire, or solder in it
  • the future we were promised. bring a piece of it, working or not
